## Version PC (Estimé Printemps 2027)

### Pourquoi le Délai?

**Industrie Standard:**
- GTA V: 8 mois (Sep 2013 → May 2014)
- GTA IV: 18 mois (Oct 2008 → Nov 2009)
- GTA 6: Probablement 6-12 mois

**Raisons Techniques:**
1. Console optimization d'abord
2. PC port = driver optimization (NVIDIA, AMD)
3. Testing 10,000+ PC configs
4. Server stability jour-1 prioritized
