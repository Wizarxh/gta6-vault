13 years of GTA Online. Billions in virtual currency earned, heists completed, cars collected.

Then November 19, 2026 arrives, and GTA 6 launches with its own online mode.

**The question every GTA Online veteran is asking:** Do I lose everything?

Here's what we know—and what we're still waiting on.

---

## Official Position (VERIFIED)

Rockstar has **not confirmed** character or wealth carry-over.

**What They've Said:**
> "GTA Online will continue to be updated on GTA V. GTA 6 will have its own Online mode with dedicated infrastructure." 
> — Rockstar Games, Official Statement

**What That Means:**
- GTA V Online and GTA 6 Online are **separate games**
- Your GTA V progress is **not automatically transferred**
- Rockstar is rebuilding from the ground up

**Why This Matters:**
This is the **industry standard** for generational game transitions. You don't carry over progression—you start fresh. But Rockstar could offer incentives for legacy players.

## Why Carry-Over Is Unlikely

### Technical Reasons

**Economy Rebalancing:**
- GTA V Online economy is inflated (modded accounts, billion-GTA$ players)
- GTA 6 Online needs a **clean economy**
- Carrying over would corrupt day-1 economy

**Service Consolidation:**
- GTA V Online stays on legacy engines (PS4/Xbox One)
- GTA 6 Online is PS5/Xbox Series X only
- No backwards compatibility in architecture

### Game Design Reasons

**Fresh Start = Engagement Hook:**
- "Everyone starts at Level 1" is motivating
- Eliminates gatekeeping (new players aren't behind veterans)
- Accelerates community cohesion

**Rockstar's Track Record:**
- Every GTA Online generation starts fresh
- No precedent for carry-over
- It's the industry standard
