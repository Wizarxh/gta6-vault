## Physics: Everything Breaks (For Real)

### Environmental Destruction

**VERIFIED (from leaked footage):**
- Glass windows shatter realistically
- Wood breaks into splinters, not chunks
- Metal deforms (not just disappears)
- Concrete cracks radiating from impact points
- Vehicles leave dents and damage trails

**How It Works:**
Each material has a **destruction model**. Hit a store window at speed? Glass shatters into 100+ individual shards, with physics applied to each. Crash through a wooden fence? It splinters realistically.

**Why It Matters:**
GTA V's destruction was binary (exists/destroyed). GTA 6's is **gradual and physical**. This means:
- Chase scenes through storefronts feel consequential
- Physics-based puzzle solving (destroy specific supports)
- Environmental storytelling (damaged buildings tell stories)

**Technical Achievement:**
This requires processing 10,000+ physics objects simultaneously. Only possible with next-gen CPU/GPU (PS5/Xbox Series X).

### Ragdoll & Character Physics

**VERIFIED:**
- NPCs ragdoll realistically on impact
- Limb collision detection (hit an arm ≠ full-body knockback)
- Realistic falling and tumbling
- Weight distribution in physics calculations

**Gameplay Implication:**
Combat feels **more skill-based**. Where you hit matters. Punch a standing NPC in the legs? They stumble. In the head? Different animation. Grab from behind? The physics account for leverage.

**RUMOR (High Confidence):**
- Vehicle hit detection changes based on speed
- Hitting pedestrians at different speeds produces different results
- Physics-driven combat (environmental kills using objects/momentum)

## Vehicles: Driving Has A Physics Sandbox

### Revolutionary Vehicle Physics

**VERIFIED from leaked footage:**
- Weight distribution affects handling
- Tire grip varies by surface (wet asphalt ≠ dry asphalt)
- Suspension deforms realistically
- Vehicle center-of-gravity visible in cornering
- Weight transfer during acceleration/braking

**Real-World Example:**
In GTA V, all cars handle similarly (arcade physics). In GTA 6:
- Light sports car = twitchy, fast, low grip
- Heavy truck = sluggish, high grip, slow turn-in
- Motorcycle = sensitive to road surface, wheelies possible

**Why It Matters:**
Driving stops being "point at destination, hold accelerator." It becomes a **skill system**. Mastering different vehicles requires learning their physics.

### Damage Model Evolution

**VERIFIED:**
- Vehicles sustain **persistent damage** through gameplay
- Doors can be knocked off separately
- Hoods, trunks dent and become non-functional
- Windows break in stages (cracks → broken → missing)
- Wheels can flatten, affecting handling

**RUMOR (Leaked but unconfirmed):**
- Severe damage triggers real-time physics failures (wheels fall off, engines catch fire)
- Repair mechanics (bring vehicle to shop or DIY)
- Performance degrades with damage (top speed, acceleration affected)
