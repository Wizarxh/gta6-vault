## PC Version (Estimated Spring 2027)

### Why the Delay?

**Industry Standard:**
- GTA V took 8 months (Sep 2013 → May 2014)
- GTA IV took 18 months (Oct 2008 → Nov 2009)
- GTA 6 will likely take 6-12 months

**Technical Reasons:**
1. Console optimization finished first
2. PC port requires driver optimization (NVIDIA, AMD)
3. Testing across 10,000+ PC configs
4. Day-1 server stability prioritized (console)

---

### PC Performance Tiers (Speculation)

#### Tier 1: High-End PC (RTX 4090 / RX 7900 XTX)

**Target Settings:**
- 4K resolution
- 120+ FPS
- Ray-tracing: Ultra
- DLSS 3 / FSR 3 enabled
- Max texture quality

**Real-World Performance:**
- Consistent 100-120 FPS at 4K ultra
- Ray-tracing minimal FPS hit

#### Tier 2: Mid-Range PC (RTX 4070 / RX 6800)

**Target Settings:**
- 1440p resolution
- 100+ FPS
- Ray-tracing: High
- DLSS 2 / FSR 2 enabled
- High texture quality

**Real-World Performance:**
- 90-100 FPS at 1440p high
- 60-70 FPS at 4K high

#### Tier 3: Budget PC (RTX 3060 / RX 5700)

**Target Settings:**
- 1080p resolution
- 60 FPS
- Ray-tracing: Medium or off
- DLSS 2 enabled
- Medium texture quality

**Real-World Performance:**
- 60+ FPS at 1080p medium
- 30-40 FPS at 1440p low

#### Tier 4: Minimum (GTX 1060 / RX 5500)

**Target Settings:**
- 720p-1080p resolution
- 30-60 FPS
- Ray-tracing: Off
- DLSS enabled (if available)
- Low texture quality

**Real-World Performance:**
- 30-40 FPS at 1080p low
- 50+ FPS at 720p low
