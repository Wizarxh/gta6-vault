Privacy Policy

GTA6 Vault - Last Updated: May 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

GTA6 Vault collects minimal user data:

  • Browser Information: IP address, user agent, pages visited (via analytics)
  • TikTok Integration: When using "Share to TikTok", we request basic user info (username, profile ID) to facilitate sharing
  • Cookies: Session cookies for authentication and preferences
Important: We do NOT collect or store passwords, email addresses, or financial information.

2. How TikTok Data is Used

When you use the TikTok Share feature, we collect OAuth2 tokens authorized for:

  • user.info.basic - To display your TikTok username
  • video.list - To show your published content (optional preview)
  • video.publish - To post articles you choose to share

These tokens are used ONLY for sharing content to your TikTok account. We do not:

  • Store or log personal TikTok data
  • Share tokens with third parties
  • Access private messages or followers
  • Monetize your content

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do NOT share your data with third parties.

Exceptions:

  • TikTok: When you explicitly share content, TikTok receives the article data and your publication
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law

4. Data Retention

  • TikTok Tokens: Stored securely; deleted when you revoke access
  • Analytics Data: Retained for up to 90 days
  • Session Data: Cleared when you log out

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • HTTPS encryption for all connections
  • Environment variable protection for API keys
  • No sensitive data stored in cookies

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request what data we hold about you
  • Delete: Request removal of your data
  • Revoke: Disconnect your TikTok account anytime
