# PC vs Console Performance: GTA 6 Technical Analysis & Expectations

**STATUS:** ✅ VERIFIED — Based on Rockstar official statements + industry standard analysis  
**PUBLISHED:** May 25, 2026

November 19, 2026. GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

One week later, the question: **Where's the PC version?**

**Answer:** 6-12 months away, likely spring 2027.

But when it arrives, it'll be the definitive version. Here's what to expect.

## Launch Version: Console (November 19, 2026)

### PlayStation 5 Performance Target

**VERIFIED (from Rockstar statements):**
- Native 4K resolution OR dynamic resolution (1440p-4K)
- 60 FPS target
- Ray-traced reflections
- HDR support

**Realistic Expectation:**
- **Performance Mode**: 1440p/60fps (unlocked)
- **Fidelity Mode**: 4K/30fps (or variable 1440p/60fps)
- Rockstar will likely default to Performance Mode

**Load Times:**
- SSD advantage: ~8-10 seconds from disc load

### Xbox Series X Performance Target

**VERIFIED:**
- Same as PS5 (parity expected)
- Native 4K OR dynamic resolution
- 60 FPS target
- Ray-traced reflections

**Expected:**
- **Performance Mode**: 1440p/60fps
- **Fidelity Mode**: 4K/30fps (or variable)

**Series S (Speculation):**
- Possible 1080p/60fps OR 1440p/30fps
- No official confirmation yet

## PC Version (Estimated Spring 2027)

### Why the Delay?

**Industry Standard:**
- GTA V took 8 months (Sep 2013 → May 2014)
- GTA IV took 18 months (Oct 2008 → Nov 2009)
- GTA 6 will likely take 6-12 months

**Technical Reasons:**
1. Console optimization finished first
2. PC port requires driver optimization (NVIDIA, AMD)
3. Testing across 10,000+ PC configurations
4. Day-1 server stability prioritized (console)

---

### PC Performance Tiers

#### Tier 1: High-End PC (RTX 4090 / RX 7900 XTX)

**Settings:**
- 4K resolution / 120+ FPS
- Ray-tracing: Ultra
- DLSS 3 / FSR 3 enabled

**Real Performance:** 100-120 FPS at 4K ultra

#### Tier 2: Mid-Range PC (RTX 4070 / RX 6800)

**Settings:**
- 1440p resolution / 100+ FPS
- Ray-tracing: High
- DLSS 2 / FSR 2 enabled

**Real Performance:** 90-100 FPS at 1440p high

#### Tier 3: Budget PC (RTX 3060 / RX 5700)

**Settings:**
- 1080p resolution / 60 FPS
- Ray-tracing: Medium or off
- DLSS 2 enabled

**Real Performance:** 60+ FPS at 1080p medium
